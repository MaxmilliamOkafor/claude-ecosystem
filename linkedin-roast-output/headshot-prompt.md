# Headshot prompt

No image tool that works from a reference photo is connected, so nothing was generated and no face was edited. Paste the prompt below into the image tool of your choice and attach your own photo.

## Prompt

Create a clean, professional LinkedIn headshot of the person in the attached photo. Keep their real face, features, skin tone and hairstyle exactly as they are: it must look like them. Shoulders up, facing the camera with a relaxed, confident expression. Plain neutral light-grey or soft blue-grey background, soft even light, no harsh shadows. Smart-casual clothes that suit a cloud and AI solutions architect at a large tech company: a plain dark shirt or a blazer over an open collar, no logos. Square crop with the face in the middle.

## Before you upload

- LinkedIn says your profile photo must reflect your likeness. Don't let a tool change your face. If the result looks like someone else, don't use it.
- Use a square image of at least 400 x 400 pixels (LinkedIn accepts up to 7680 x 4320), JPG or PNG, under 8 MB.
- Your photo goes to whichever image tool you use. Read its privacy terms first.
